The Window Manager Experiment#

Recently, I've been experimenting with dynamic and scrollable tiling environments like Niri and Umbriel. Instead of traditional fixed binary trees, an infinite horizontal ribbon brings a tangible mental model to multi-monitor workflows.

Pro Tip: Garbage Collection

Run nix-collect-garbage --delete-older-than 14d bi-weekly to prevent store inflation while retaining instant rollback capability.

Warning on Proprietary Drivers

When updating kernel trees on hybrid graphics laptops, ensure your Nvidia/Mesa modules match upstream revision tags before rebuilding your generation.

Critical Rule

Never commit unencrypted secrets or GPG private keys directly into git-tracked flake trees. Always rely on sops-nix or agenix.
