## The Window Manager Experiment

Recently, I've been experimenting with dynamic and scrollable tiling environments like [Niri](https://github.com/YaLTeR/niri) and Umbriel. Instead of traditional fixed binary trees, an infinite horizontal ribbon brings a tangible mental model to multi-monitor workflows.

<Callout type="note" title="Compositor Observation">
Scrolling tiling feels drastically more natural on ultra-wide panels. Windows remain at their ideal column widths without manual resizing gymnastics.
</Callout>

## Callout Variations

Here is how different callout types render for important alerts, security notices, and quotes:

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip: Garbage Collection">
Run `nix-collect-garbage --delete-older-than 14d` bi-weekly to prevent store inflation while retaining instant rollback capability.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Warning on Proprietary Drivers">
When updating kernel trees on hybrid graphics laptops, ensure your Nvidia/Mesa modules match upstream revision tags before rebuilding your generation.
</Callout>

<Callout type="danger" title="Critical Rule">
Never commit unencrypted secrets or GPG private keys directly into git-tracked flake trees. Always rely on `sops-nix` or `agenix`.
</Callout>
